Selection Process Design

It's a legal, structural, and cultural problem with recruiting on top. I've built people functions across the US, Canada, UK, Germany, Singapore, and Asia-Pacific. I know which decisions are reversible and which ones aren't, and I know how to hire before you have a legal entity in market.
