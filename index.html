If we reached out, here's who you're talking to.

SoundHerald works with a small number of high-growth companies to find their senior engineering, product, and design leaders. We don't send out roles in bulk or work from keyword searches. If we get in touch, it's because someone who understands the work thinks you're worth a conversation.

We don't work with everyone. We take on a few searches at a time, for teams we'd be proud to introduce you to.

What happens next

  1. A 30-minute conversation. We walk through the team, the problem, and the role, and hear what you're looking for. Even if it's not a match, you'll leave with a candid read on the market.
  2. The full picture before you decide. You get the level, scope, reporting line, and comp range before anything goes further.
  3. Your call. Everything stays confidential, and we never introduce you to a company without your go-ahead.
  4. We close the loop. Whatever happens, you'll hear back from us.

Hire well

Where we help early-stage and high-growth companies get the right people in.

Executive Search

We find people who aren't looking, tell you the truth about what we're seeing, and close them.

We've run C-suite and senior searches across the US, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. We don't charge agency percentage fees or rush a search before the fit is right. Our team has made these calls at Block and Shopify, and we know what a great hire at your stage actually looks like.

Selection Process Design

Speed kills hiring quality.

When you're moving fast, gut feel takes over and standards drift. We design interview processes that stay structured without slowing you down, covering competency frameworks, scorecards, documented criteria, and calibration sessions. It's the kind of infrastructure that let Shopify fill thousands of roles in a single year and still know exactly what they were hiring for.

Global Expansion

Hiring in a new country starts well before recruiting.

Legal, structural, and cultural decisions come first. We've built people functions across the US, Canada, UK, Germany, Singapore, and wider Asia-Pacific. We know which decisions are reversible and which ones aren't, and how to hire before you have a legal entity in market.

Plan, pay, and keep

The structure that holds up at your next round.

Workforce Planning and Unit Economics

Know who to hire first, what they should produce, and what it costs if they don't.

We build headcount models tied to real business logic, including revenue per employee targets, cost per hire ratios, and capacity models by function. When you're in front of your board, you'll have the numbers.

Compensation and Equity Frameworks

Every shortcut you take on comp now becomes a problem at the next round.

We build the full structure: job levels, salary bands, equity philosophy, vesting, refresh cadences, and acceleration provisions. The goal is to compete for great people without leaving a mess on your cap table.

Retention and People Infrastructure

Most resignations start weeks before anyone gives notice.

We put practical habits in place to catch issues early: solid onboarding, straightforward performance reviews, benefits and LTIP design, and people metrics that show what's going on across your team.

Tools and AI

Deciding what to buy, what to build, and what to skip.

AI in HR and People Workflows

Most HR tech AI tools aren't ready for real use.

We build internal HR AI tools ourselves, including screening, onboarding, reporting, and retention systems, so we can tell what holds up in practice versus a sales demo. We help you evaluate vendors, spot where automation genuinely helps, and skip tools that create more work than they save.

HR and Talent Tech Stack

The vendor will always tell you their tool is right for your stage.

We've evaluated and implemented most of the major platforms at scale across ATS, HRIS, performance management, L&D, fraud detection, and referral tools. We'll tell you what to buy, what to wait on, and what to consider building yourself.
